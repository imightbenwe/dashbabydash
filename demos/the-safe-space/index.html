I’m Nina, and I combine training in SOMA breathwork, somatics, and trauma release to create gentle, effective journeys back to wholeness.

My mission is to create a safe, authentic space where people can reconnect to their hearts. I know this work because I’ve lived it. I’ve healed my own wounds and ADHD patterns through these practices.

Through somatic work, breath, movement, touch, sound, and meditation, I guide you to meet yourself fully, transform stagnant energy, and awaken your inner pharmacy.

The Healing Trinity

We combine three powerful modalities to help you drop out of your head and into your body.

SOMA Breath®

Rhythmic, music-driven breathwork that encourages efficient oxygen-CO₂ exchange. It boosts energy, lowers oxidative stress, and creates a state of coherence between heart and mind.

Somatic Healing

Trauma isn't just in the mind; it lives in the body. We use gentle movement, touch, and awareness to release the "6 belts of tension" and signal safety to the vagus nerve.

Holotropic Style

Intensive conscious breathing for profound personal exploration. Supported by evocative music, this modality accesses non-ordinary states for deep emotional release.
