Meet Holi

I believe that every human deserves a life rooted in connection.

For the past 10 years, I have dedicated myself to de-conditioning and unlearning societal expectations placed on women. I dove into shadow work, meeting the deepest and darkest parts of myself, eventually finding a beautiful and abundant light.

Ten years ago, I heard the words: "You have a choice." In that moment, I realized how disconnected I had become—numb to life, my heart encased in stone. I had walled myself off from the very essence of living: love, connection, & understanding.

My internal devotion to self became a devotion to others. We all deserve safe spaces where we can be witnessed and held. To create true self-love by tapping into our inner somatic communication.
