The Founder

Hi, I'm Luce.

In my early twenties, I discovered yoga and fell in love with the mind-body connection it brought. It opened me up to deeper questions: What does it mean to live a good life?

I became a health coach and a yoga teacher, and in 2020, Inner States was born. Life has shown me that things don't always go as planned, but having trusted tools—especially when you're stressed or feeling flat—makes all the difference.

As for me? My essentials are yoga, a steady routine, beach walks, time with family, pizza, and laughing often.

Luce x
